How we handle alternatives and price

Rules that stay the same from lot to lot.

Original where it matters

For technologically complex, project-specific and safety-critical equipment we supply the original only.

Alternatives of equal grade only

For simple items we offer an equivalent from a proven manufacturer — to cut cost, not quality.

One offer, one price

We submit one offer with a final price. It does not change after submission.
